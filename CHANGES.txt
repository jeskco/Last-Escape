CHANGES IN THIS VERSION (3_12, built on 3_11)

  * AUTO-AIM IS NO LONGER IN THE WAY OF MOVING. The game turns your character to face whatever it is aiming at,
    and walking away from the way you face is "backwards": slow, no sprinting, and with a heavy load it can be
    zero. So when auto-aim grabbed an enemy and you pushed the other way, you could not move or sprint.
    Now, while you are pushing the move stick, you face the way you are walking (full speed, sprint works).
    Auto-aim only snaps to an enemy when you are standing still or holding FIRE.
  * Only game.swf changed (index.html and everything else are identical to 3_11).

NOT TESTED in the real game. The game script compiled and decompiled OK and every other part of game.swf is
identical to 3_11.
If holding FIRE while backing away feels wrong (you walk slowly because you keep facing the enemy), tell me
and I can change that too.
